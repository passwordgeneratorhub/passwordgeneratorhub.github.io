Why use a password generator?

Humans pick predictable passwords

People reuse names, dates and keyboard patterns. A generator picks uniformly at random, so attackers cannot guess your habits.

Length beats cleverness

Every extra character multiplies the guessing work. A 16-character random password is vastly stronger than an 8-character "clever" one.

Private by design

These tools have no server and no tracking. Your passwords are generated locally and never uploaded, stored or logged.

Pair it with a manager

Generate a unique password for every account and store it in a password manager, so you only have to remember one strong master password.

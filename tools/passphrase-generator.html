Passphrase Generator

Passphrases are easier to remember than random strings and can be just as strong. Pick a word count and a separator.

Words are drawn at random with crypto.getRandomValues from a built-in word list. Four random words from a 100-word list give about 27 bits; use five or six words for important accounts.
